CHUYÊN ĐỀ: MỆNH ĐỀ QUAN HỆ

I. Combine each pair of sentences using a relative pronoun or relative adverb

1. She is the most intelligent woman. I’ve ever met this woman.

____She is the most intelligent woman that I’ve ever met this woman. ______________________

2. This doctor is famous. You visited him yesterday.

___The doctor whom you visited yesterday is famous.__________

3. These children are orphans. She is taking care of these children.

___The children ,whom she is talking care of, are orphans._________________

4. The two young men are not good persons. You are acquainted with them.

___The two young men whom you are acquainted with are not good persons.____________

5. My father goes swimming every day. You met him this morning.

___My father whom you met this morning goes swimming every day.______

6. The man is my father. I respect this man most.

____The man whom I respect most is my father.__________

7. The man is my father. I respect his opinion most.

___The man whose opinion I respect most is my father._________________

8. Mary and Margaret are twins. You met them yesterday.

____Mary and Margaret, whom you met yesterday, are twins._____________

9. I’ll introduce you to the man. His support is necessary for your project.

___I’ll introduce you to the man whose support is necessary for your project._______________

10. The middle-aged man is the director. My father is talking to him.

____The middle-aged man, whom my father is talking to, is the director._______

11. The boy is my cousin. You make fun of him.

____The boy whom you make fun of is my cousin.__________

12. The student is from China. He sits next to me.

___The student who sits next to me is from China.____________

13. I thanked the woman. This woman had helped me.

______I thanked the woman who had helped me.________________

14. The professor is excellent. I am taking his course.

____The professor whose course I am talking is excellent.___________

15. Mr. Smith said he was too busy to speak to me. I had come to see him.

_____Mr. Smith, whom I had come to see, said he was too busy to speak to me._____________

16. I saw a lot of people and horses. They went to market.

___I saw a lot of people and horses that went to market._____

17. Tom has three sisters. All of them are married.

____Tom has three sister, all of whom are married.__________

18. I recently went back to Paris. It is still as beautiful as a pearl.

____I recently went back to Paris, which is still as beautiful as a pearl._______

19. I recently went back to Paris. I was born in Paris nearly 50 years ago.

I recently went back to Paris where I was been nearly 50 years ago.

20. Do you know the music? It is being played on the radio.

_____Do you know the music which is being played on the radio.__________

21. You didn’t tell us the reason. We have to cut down our daily expenses for that reason.

You didn’t tell us the reason why we have to cut down our daily expenses.

22. The day was rainy. She left on that day.

___The day when she left was rainy.______________

23. I’ve sent him two letters. He has received neither of them.

____I’ve sent him two letters, neither of which he has received._________

24. That man is an artist. I don’t remember the man’s name.

___That man whose name I don’t remember is an artist._______________

25. One of the elephants had only one tusk. We saw these elephants at the zoo.

We saw these elephants at the zoo, one of which had only one tusk.

26. That car belongs to Dr. Clark. Its engine is very good.

___That car whose engine is very good belongs to Dr. Clark._________________

27. You sent me a present. Thank you very much for it.

___Thank you very much for the present which you sent me.________

28. This is Mrs. Jones. Her son won the championship last year.

____This is Mrs. Jones, whose son won the championship last year.______________

29. Rod Lee has won an Oscar. I know his sister.

_____Rod Lee, whose sister I know, has won an Oscar.____________

30. Is this the style of hair? Your wife wants to have it.

___Is this the style of hair which your wife wants to have ?___________

31. A man answered the phone. He said Tom was out.

___A man who answered the phone said Tom was out ._____________

32. 7.05 is the time. My plane arrives then.

____7.05 is the time when my plane arrives._________________

33. Max isn’t home yet. That worries me.

____Max isn’t home yet, which worries me._________________

34. Do you know the building? The windows of the building are painted green.

____Do you know the building the windows of which are pained green ?_____________

35. Last week I went to see the house. I used to live in it.

Last week I went to see the house where I used to live._______________

36. I don’t know the girl’s name. She’s just gone into the hall.

___I don’t know the name of the girl who has just gone into the hall. __________________

37. Be sure to follow the instructions. They are given at the top of the page.

___Be sure to follow the instructions which are given at the top of the page ._______

38. Hoan Kiem lake is a historical place. Its water is always blue.

______Hoan Kiem lake whose water is always blue is a historical place.____________

Hoan Kiem lake, the water of which is always blue, is a historical place.

II. Fill in the blanks with Relative pronouns or Relative adverbs where necessary

1. Let me see all the letters __which_____ you have written.

2. Is there any one __who______ can help me do this?

3. Mr. Brown, ___who______ is only 34, is the director of this company.

4. I know a place ___where_______ roses grow in abundance.

5. It was the nurse __who______ told me to come in.

6. The teacher with ___whom_____, we studied last year no longer teaches in our school.

7. They showed me the hospital ___where_____ buildings had been destroyed by US bombings.

8. We saw many soldiers and tanks __that____ were moving to the front.

9. Dr. Fleming, __who_____ discovered penicillin, was awarded the Nobel Prize for medicine in 1945.

10. He joined the political party ___which____ was in power.

11. Love, _which_______ is a wonderful feeling, comes to everyone at some time in his life.

12. Freedom is something for __which_____ millions have given their lives.

13. It is easy to find faults in people ___whom_____ we dislike.

14. The really happy people are those _who______ enjoy their daily work.

15. We must find a time ____when___ we can meet and a place __where_____ we can talk.
