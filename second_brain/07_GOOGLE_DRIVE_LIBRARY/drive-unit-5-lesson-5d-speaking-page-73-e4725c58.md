Suggested Answer Key

A Saturday afternoon

A: Hi! How’s it going?

B: Not bad. How about you?

A: I’m fine.

B: Do you have any plans for the weekend?

A: Nothing special. Why?

B: I’m going to listen to music on Saturday

afternoon. Do you want to come, too?

A: That doesn’t sound like fun! I don’t like

listening to music very much. How about

watching TV instead?

B: That’s a good idea! I like watching TV.

A: Is 2 p.m. OK with you?

B: It’s great.

A: OK. See you then.

B. Sunday morning

A: Hi! How’s it going?

B: Not bad. How about you?

A: I’m fine.

B: Do you have any plans for the weekend?

A: Nothing special. Why?

B: I’m going to play computer games on Sunday

morning. Do you want to play with me?

A: That doesn’t sound like fun! I don’t like

playing computer games. How about

playing sports instead?

B: That’s a great idea! I like playing sports.

A: Is 10 a.m. OK with you?

B: It’s great. Let’s meet at the park.

A: See you there.

a. Objectives: To learn and practise sentence stress

b. Content: Task Pronunciation (CD 2.21)

c. Expected outcomes: To practice pronunciation- sentence stress.

d. Organisation:

Teacher’s activities

Students’ activities

- Explain the task and play the recording.

- Give Ss time to underline the sentence stress.

-  Check Ss’ answers.

-  Play the recording with pauses for Ss to listen

and repeat chorally and/or individually and

then have Ss practise saying the sentences

with their partner.

- Pay attention to Ss’ pronunciation and give

corrections if necessary

- Listen and pay attention.

- Underline the words.

- Work in pairs to check.

- Listen and repeat chorally and individually.

- Practise with classmates.
