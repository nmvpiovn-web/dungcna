## Nội dung tài liệu

Rewrite the following sentences in Reported Speech (Statements/Questions)

Nam said: "I am told to be at school before 7 o'clock".

…Nam said that he was told to be at school before 7 o’clock………….

Thu said: "All the students will have a meeting next week".

…Thu said that all the students would have a meeting the folloing week…

Phong said: “My parents are very proud of my good marks".

…Phong said that his parents were very proud of his good marks…

The teacher said: "All the homework must be done carefully".

…The teachers said that all the homework had to be done carefully…

Her father said to her: "You can go to the movie with your friend".

…Her father told her she could go to the movie with her friend……

"Do you enjoy reading?", Phong asked Peter.

…Phong asked Peter if he enjoyed reading……

"Do your sister and brother go to the same school?", she asked Nam.

…She asked Nam if his sister and brother went to the sae school……

"Are there any oranges in the fridge?", she asked her mom.

…She asked her mom if there were any oranges in the fridge…

"Were you reading this book at 8 o'clock last Sunday?", she asked Ba.

…She asked Ba if he had been reading that book at 8 o’clock the Sunday before.

"Will it rain tomorrow morning?", he asked his friend.

…He asked his friend if it would rain the next day morning …

"Where does your father work?" the teacher asked me.

…The teacher asked me where my father worked……

"How many people are there in your family?" she asked Lan.

…She asked Lan how many people there were in her family……

The teacher said to Lien: "What's your hobby?"

…The teacher asked Lien what her hobby was.

"How do you go to the airport?" his friend asked him.

…His friend asked him how he went to the airport…

"I wish I hadn't gone to the party last night" she said.

…She said that she wished she hadn’t gone to the party the night before …

" The Earth moves around the Sun", my teacher said.

…My teacher said that the Earth moved around the Sun……

18. Peter said: "I want to tell you the news. You must be surprised"

…Peter said that he wanted to tell me the news and I had to be surprised………

The woman said: “When I was walking on the pavement, a strange man stopped and asked me the way to the nearest bank."

…The woman said that when she had been walking on the pavement, a strange had stopped and had asked her the wayto the nearest bank …

Rewrite the following sentences in Reported Speech (Conditional in reported speech)

He said, “If I catch the plane, I’ll be home by five.”

…He said that if he caught the plane, he would be home by five………

He said, “If I had a permit, I could get a job.”

…He said that if he had had a permit, he could get a job………

“If she had loved Tom,” he said, “she wouldn’t have left him.”

He said that if she had loved Tom, she wouldn’t have left him………

He said, “If you have time what will you do?”

…He said that if I had time what I would do………

She said, “If you want to lose weight, you must eat less bread.”
…She said that if I wanted to lose weight, I had to eat less bread……

He said, “If I had a map, I would lend it to you.”
…He said that if he had had a map, he would lend it to me……

He said, “If I had known that you were coming, I would have met you at the airport.”

…He said that if he had known that I had been coming, he would have met me at the airport …

He wondered, “If the baby is the girl what will they call her?”

…He wondered if the baby was the girl what they would call her……

She said, “If you feel ill, why don’t you go to bed?”

…She said that if I felt ill, why I didn’t go to bed……
